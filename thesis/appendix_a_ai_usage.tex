\chapter{Generative AI Tools Usage}
\label{genAIUsage}

ChatGPT (OpenAI; versions 5.6 Sol and 6 Astra) was used as a supplementary assistant for literature exploration, language editing, \LaTeX{} formatting, and technical troubleshooting. Suggested publications were verified against the original academic sources before citation. Proposed textual, formatting, and technical changes were reviewed by the author and, where applicable, checked through compilation or software tests before use.

Generative AI did not determine the research questions, methods, system-design decisions, evaluation protocol, interpretation of results, or conclusions. Responsibility for the submitted text, implementation, evidence, and claims remains with the author.
